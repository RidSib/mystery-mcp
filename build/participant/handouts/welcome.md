# Welcome to the Academy

Work in teams of three or four. The session is 75 minutes: 15 minutes to build your second brain, then 60 minutes to investigate the case the facilitator introduces.

Connect with the details on your team card. Inspect a tool request and result. Keep findings, sources, and open questions in your own Markdown wiki. An AI answer is a proposal to check against evidence.

## Search boundary

Some clues are hidden around this room and in the bathrooms. Finding them never requires opening, unplugging or moving equipment, and nothing is hidden in the real server room, desks, bins, offices or stairwells. Photograph what you find; don’t take it.

## Team rules

- Photograph clues; leave them in place for other teams.
- Keep your team's workspace and passcodes private.
- At minute 50, start a fresh AI conversation from your saved wiki, including room photos or transcriptions. Keep your passcodes in the wiki so the new conversation can use them.
- You have two counted accusations, handed in on paper.
- Cite the evidence behind your answer. The case brief will explain minimum completion and full credit.

You can continue retrieving records after the conversation reset, as long as your AI sends your passcodes.
