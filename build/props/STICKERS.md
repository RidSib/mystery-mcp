# Sticker plan — facilitator only

All clues are **removable stickers**, colour-coded by **location**, not by importance. Real clues and decoys share a colour at each spot, so colour says nothing about what matters.

| Colour | Location | Real | Decoy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟠 Orange | Behind the main screen | Vault note (`real/screen-note.md`) | "server room: 1234 — T.E." |
| 🔵 Blue | Bathroom (copy in every bathroom) | Costume shop label | Grey Lecturer riddle, cleaning rota |
| 🟢 Green | Side of the fridge | Drinks-service sticker, Christmas party list, HustleHub card | #ghosthunt |

**Before the event**
- Use removable stickers that won't mark the screen or the machine.
- Print or write a full spare set; stickers get peeled off. A lost sticker is easy to replace: its colour tells you where it belongs.
- Check the back of the main screen is reachable without unplugging anything.
- Read out: photograph what you find; don't take it.

Totals: 5 real stickers (+ a costume label per extra bathroom), 4 decoys.
